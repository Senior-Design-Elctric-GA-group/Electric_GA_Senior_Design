Airport data

Download airports.csv and runways.csv from ourairports.com/data (public domain), then select both, or drop them on the map. Parsing runs in this browser and the result is kept for your next visit. Any CSV with latitude and longitude columns also works.

Method

Distances are great-circle distances on a sphere of mean radius 3,440.07 nmi (haversine); the difference from the WGS-84 ellipsoid stays below 0.5%.

Each range circle is traced on the sphere with the destination-point formula (up to 256 vertices) and only then projected to Web Mercator, so circles stretch correctly toward the poles and wrap across the antimeridian.

Combined coverage is the union of all circles. The network view links airports within one usable range of each other; colours mark groups that are mutually reachable with recharging at every stop.

OurAirports has no public/private field, so use is inferred from identifiers: ICAO, IATA and 3-character FAA codes count as public; FAA codes like CA12 and locally assigned IDs count as private.
